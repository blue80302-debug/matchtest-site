Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

At Love Match Test ("we", "us", or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide and improve our service:

  • Names entered into the test: The names you type are processed entirely in your web browser (client-side). They are never transmitted to or stored on any server.
  • IP-based geolocation: We use a third-party service (ipapi.co) to detect your approximate country for language auto-detection. We do not store your IP address.
  • Analytics data: We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. This may include anonymized data such as page views, browser type, device type, and approximate location.
  • Advertising data: If advertisements are displayed on our site (e.g., Google AdSense), third-party ad networks may collect cookies and usage data to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the name compatibility test functionality.
  • To automatically detect and set the appropriate display language.
  • To analyze website traffic and improve user experience.
  • To display relevant advertisements that support the free operation of this site.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our site may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — for analytics and advertising purposes. Third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense may set their own cookies. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

  • ipapi.co — For IP-based geolocation (language detection).
  • Google Fonts — For web font delivery.
  • Kakao SDK — For KakaoTalk sharing functionality (optional).
  • Google AdSense — For displaying advertisements (if enabled).
  • Google Analytics — For website usage analytics (if enabled).

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their policies for details on their data practices.

5. Data Security

Since all name processing occurs entirely within your browser, your personal data (names entered) never leaves your device. We do not operate databases that store user-entered names. We implement reasonable security measures to protect any data collected through analytics and advertising services.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of cookies and tracking (via browser settings).
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
