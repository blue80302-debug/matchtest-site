Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the compatibility score real?
A: No! Love Match Test is purely for entertainment. The scores are generated by a mathematical algorithm and have no scientific basis. Have fun with it, but don't take it too seriously!

Q: Do you store the names I enter?
A: No. All name processing happens entirely in your browser. We never see, transmit, or store the names you enter.

Q: Can I use Love Match Test on my phone?
A: Yes! The site is fully responsive and works on all devices — smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Q: Why do I get different scores for the same names in different tools?
A: Different name compatibility tools use different algorithms. Our algorithm uses Unicode-based character values for universal language support, which may produce different results from tools that use stroke-count or letter-position methods.
